name: add-customer
description: Add a customer to Economico with a contract on one of the company's plans.
---

Add the customer the founder names: invoke the `manage-sales` skill and follow its `customer-contracts` reference, with the signed order form, checkout or accepted terms as the source. If the price they agreed is not one of the company's plans, invoke `product-economics` and see its `modeling-products` reference before binding them.
